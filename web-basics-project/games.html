Video Games

My Bird

If you're wondering why there's a bird here it's because I have a cockatiel named Pichu! She's named after one of my favorite videogames, Pokémon™.

She usually hangs out with me while I'm on my laptop, either while I'm gaming or I'm drawing. When I'm back at my parents' house, she'll sit with me on the couch while I play games on the TV!

Game Design

Not only do I love playing games, but I enjoy making them. I've dabbled in some game design, and have made one or two working video games. My dream is to one day gather a group of friends and create a full game together!

Though they don't technically fall under the umbrella of "video game", I also love playing tabletop role-playing games (TTRPGs) with my friends. I'm also just generally a fan of board games and puzzles!
