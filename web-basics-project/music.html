Music


Instruments

I've always been interested in making music, though more as a fun side hobby if anything. I'm pretty competent on the piano and guitar, and my former boss gifted me a glockenspiel with hopes I'd be part of an "archives band" at my workplace (I worked at my college's archives).

I love to sing, so I try an attend karaoke every Monday at my local bar. My favorite part of karaoke is when everyone sings together, and I have to say that it's one of the best ways to experience Queen's Bohemian Rhapsody.

Musical Failures

This year, 2026, some friends and I got together and decided to make some absolutely terrible music. We did. And it was absolutely terrible. You can find us on soundcloud. I personally recommend "Mosquito on my Eyeball."

Music is cool

Like most people, I'm a fan of music. I honestly can't say there's a genre of music I don't like. That being said, I typically listen to alt rock, orchestral pop, rap, metal, gothic country, and more...
